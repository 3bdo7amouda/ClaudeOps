## Headline Formulas
```
[Number] ways to [achieve outcome] without [pain point]
How [audience] [achieve result] in [timeframe]
The [adjective] way to [achieve outcome]
Stop [doing painful thing]. Start [doing desired thing].
[Outcome] for [audience] who [qualifier]
```

## Value Proposition Structure
```
We help [audience]
achieve [outcome]
by [unique mechanism]
unlike [alternative].
```

## CTA Principles
- First-person: "Start my free trial" > "Start your free trial"
- Be specific: "Get the report" > "Download"
- Reduce friction: "Try free" > "Sign up"
- One CTA per section — don't split attention

## Email Structure
```
Subject: [Curiosity/Benefit/Urgency] — under 50 chars
Preview: extends subject, don't repeat it

Body:
  Hook (1 sentence — why they should keep reading)
  Problem (2-3 sentences — agitate the pain)
  Solution (your product/offer)
  Proof (one stat, testimonial, or case study)
  CTA (single, clear, specific)
  PS (restate the offer or urgency — most read after subject)
```

## Landing Page Sections (order matters)
```
1. Hero — headline + subhead + CTA + social proof
2. Problem — name the pain clearly
3. Solution — your product as the answer
4. Features → Benefits (always translate features to outcomes)
5. Proof — testimonials, logos, stats
6. FAQ — kill objections
7. Final CTA — repeat offer
```

## From: marketingskills (cold-email)
```
Writing principles:
- Write like a peer, not a vendor — contractions, direct, no marketing speak
- Every sentence must earn its place — if removing it doesn't hurt the flow, cut it
- Personalization must connect to the problem — not just a name swap
- Lead with their world: "You/your" dominates over "I/we"
- One ask, low friction — "Worth exploring?" beats "Schedule a 30-min demo"

Voice checklist:
✗ "I hope this email finds you well"
✗ "I came across your profile"
✗ "leverage," "synergy," "best-in-class"
✓ Peer-to-peer tone
✓ Problem-first, solution-second
✓ One specific CTA
```

## Email Sequence (product launch)
```
Day 0:  Welcome — what to do first (one action)
Day 1:  Key feature intro — fastest path to value
Day 3:  Social proof — customer story or stat
Day 5:  Address top objection
Day 7:  Trial ending reminder + offer
Day 10: Last chance + support offer
```

## Gotchas

1. **Features are not benefits.** "Real-time sync" is a feature. "Never lose a change when your connection drops" is a benefit. Always translate: "This means you can..." after every feature statement.

2. **Social proof placement matters.** Testimonials at the top of a landing page ("people love us!") are weak. Testimonials directly after the claim they validate are strong ("does it actually work?" → customer says yes right there).

3. **The PS line is the second-most-read part of any email** after the subject line. Never waste it on a signature. Restate the core offer or urgency.

4. **Subject lines under 40 characters outperform longer ones** on mobile. Test: read your subject line on your phone lock screen. If it's truncated, it's too long.

5. **"We" copy kills conversion.** Count "we/our" vs "you/your" — if "we" wins, rewrite. Readers don't care about you; they care about their problem.
